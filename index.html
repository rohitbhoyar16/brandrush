The Format

This isn't a workshop.
It's an ambush.

A BrandRush crew shows up somewhere with people — a market, a mall, a college gate — and pulls a random stranger into the game. From that second, the clock is theirs.

0:00

The Grab

We approach a random person on the street and ask one question: "Got 60 minutes to build a brand?" If they say yes, the timer starts immediately.

0:15

The Concept

Name, niche, and identity, decided on the spot. No overthinking allowed — the clock doesn't care about your creative block.

0:40

The Build

Logo, website, Instagram page, and launch video get built live, in public, with people watching over their shoulder.

1:00

The Launch

At zero, whatever exists goes live. Real page, real link, real brand — shipped in front of a crowd that just watched it happen.

The Hotwheels Rule

Chaos gets
rewarded.

Every time a contestant does something genuinely unhinged — pitches a stranger for feedback, films the launch video mid-traffic, renames the brand three times, talks a shopkeeper into a cameo — the crew hands them a Hotwheels car on the spot.

🏎️

One car, one moment. No cars for good design. Cars are only for doing something bold, weird, or unplanned in the middle of the build.

📸

Filmed, not judged later. The crew hands the car out live, on camera, the second it happens — there's no backroom scoring.

🏁

Most cars wins. At the end of the day's rushes, whoever's collected the most Hotwheels takes the win — not whoever has the "best" brand.

Before You Ask

Common questions
from strangers.

Do I need any design or business experience?+

No. Most contestants have never built a brand before — that's the point. BrandRush is built for people who'd normally never call themselves "creative."

What if I say no when the crew approaches me?+

Totally fine — no pressure, no follow-up. We move to the next person. If you change your mind later, just flag down the crew before we leave the area.

Do I need my own laptop or phone?+

A phone helps for the video and Instagram, but the crew always carries a laptop for the website and logo work, so you can start with nothing in hand.

What actually happens to the brand after the hour ends?+

It's yours. The website, the handle, the video — all of it stays live and belongs to you. Some contestants keep running theirs for real after the Rush ends.

How do you decide who gets a Hotwheels car?+

The crew hands one out the moment something wild happens — no pre-set checklist, no scoring after the fact. It has to be earned live, on camera.

Can I bring a friend to compete alongside me?+

Yes — some of our best Rushes have been duos. Just know the Hotwheels get counted per person, so you might end up racing each other too.
